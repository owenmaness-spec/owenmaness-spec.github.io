Grans coffee cake

This is a recipe of Gran's coffee cake, when we visit her in Oklahoma she makes this for my sister but we all love it, now we have this recipe and make it for my sisters birthday.


"A recipe from a friend of my parents that became a family favorite"


The coffee cake batter

Ingredient Amount
Yellow Cake mix One box
Instant vanilla pudding One package/3.4 oz
Butter extract 1 teaspoon
Cooking Oil 3/4 cups
Water 3/4 cups
Vanilla 1 teaspoon
Eggs 4
Bundt pan 1
  1. Mix all the ingredients above other than the vanilla, buter extract, and eggs
  2. Add eggs one at a time
  3. Mix very well for 8 minutes (if using automatic mixer turn it on high)
  4. After mixing add the butter extract and vanilla

The topping and glaze

Ingredient Amount
Sugar 1/4 Cups
Cinammon 2 teaspoons
Powdered sugar 1/2 Cups
Water Eyeball
  1. Mix the sugar and cinnamon together to create the topping
  2. Grease your bundt pan and sprinkle flour on it to stop the batter from sticking to the top when the cake is finished.
  3. Pour 1/3 batter into the pan, then 1/2 topping mixture, then 1/3 batter, then 1/2 topping, and finally 1/3 batter
  4. Bake in an oven and 350 degrees for 1 hour
  5. This is a family recipe so we usually eyeball the amount of water in the glaze, then add your 1/2 cup of powdered sugar and mix
  6. When the cake is done, flip the bundt pan over and slide the cake out carefully, apply glaze on top when the cake is warm
